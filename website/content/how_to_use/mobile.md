## Mobile Apps

For mobile apps, you can use the same styles with [MapLibre Native](https://maplibre.org/).

Offline downloads are allowed up to 3,000 tiles and 50 MB per download, with at most 4 parallel requests and a unique User-Agent. Larger areas or long routes can be saved as several separate downloads, each started by the user. For anything else, please [contact us](mailto:info@openfreemap.org).
